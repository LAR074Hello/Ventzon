This Independent Contractor and Commission Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Ventzon ("Company") and the Contractor named above ("Rep"). By signing below, both parties agree to the following terms.

1. Relationship of the Parties

1.1  Rep is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Ventzon. Rep is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received under this Agreement, including self-employment taxes.

1.2  Rep has no authority to bind Ventzon to any contract, obligation, or liability without prior written consent.

1.3  Ventzon will issue a Form 1099 to Rep if total compensation in a calendar year equals or exceeds $600.

2. Scope of Work

2.1  Rep agrees to prospect, pitch, and onboard local merchant businesses onto the Ventzon loyalty platform.

2.2  Rep is responsible for their own prospecting. No leads are guaranteed by Ventzon.

2.3  Rep must represent Ventzon's products and pricing accurately and professionally at all times.

3. Commission Structure

Rep earns commission on revenue generated from merchants they personally close. Commission is calculated as follows:

Revenue Source Rate Example
Monthly subscription ($25/mo per merchant) 50% = $12.50/mo per active merchant 50 merchants × 75 customers = $625/mo
Annual subscription ($240/yr per merchant) 50% = $120.00/yr per active merchant Paid monthly: $10.00/mo
Per-redemption fee ($0.85 per reward redeemed) 50% = $0.43 per redemption 50 stores × 18 redemptions/mo = $387/mo

3.1 Stacking.  Commission is recurring. As long as a merchant remains active and paying, Rep continues to earn their monthly commission — it stacks month over month.

3.2 Cancellations.  If a merchant cancels their subscription, commission for that merchant ceases at the end of their final billing period.

3.3 Clawback.  If a merchant cancels within 60 days of their first payment, any commission already paid for that merchant will be deducted from Rep's next payout.

3.4 Payment Schedule.  Commission is calculated and paid monthly, on or before the 15th of the following month, via [bank transfer / Venmo / check — choose one].

3.5 No Commission Cap.  There is no cap on total commission Rep may earn.

4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

4.1  Rep agrees to keep all Ventzon business information strictly confidential, including but not limited to: merchant lists, pricing, product roadmap, revenue figures, internal tools, and business strategy.

4.2  Rep may not share, sell, or use any confidential information for any purpose other than performing services under this Agreement.

4.3  This obligation survives termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

5. Non-Solicitation

5.1  During the term of this Agreement and for 12 months after termination, Rep may not directly solicit any Ventzon merchant for a competing loyalty or rewards platform.

5.2  Rep may not solicit any Ventzon employee or contractor to leave Ventzon during the same period.

6. Intellectual Property

6.1  All sales materials, scripts, and tools provided by Ventzon remain the property of Ventzon. Rep may not reproduce or distribute them outside of their sales activities for Ventzon.

7. Term & Termination

7.1  This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.

7.2  Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with 14 days written notice.

7.3  Upon termination, Rep is entitled to all earned and unpaid commission through the termination date. Recurring commission on active merchants ceases at termination unless otherwise agreed in writing.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of _______________. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration before a mutually agreed arbitrator.

9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions. Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
