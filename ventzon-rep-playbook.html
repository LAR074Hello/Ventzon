Chapter 2
The pitch

Before you walk in

Look at the business. Do they have a paper punch card on the counter? Even better — that's your opening. Do they look busy? Come back in 20 minutes. You want the owner or manager, not a cashier.

The opener (15 seconds)

SAY THIS

"Hey, I'm [Name] — I work with Ventzon. We help local businesses run a digital loyalty program. Customers scan a QR code, earn stamps, and come back for a reward. No hardware, no app to download. Takes about 5 minutes to set up. Got 2 minutes for me to show you how it looks?"

If they say yes — open the demo on your phone and walk them through it. If they say they're busy, say:

IF BUSY

"No problem at all. Here's a flyer — I'll come back [specific day/time]. What time works better for you?"

The demo (60 seconds)

Open ventzon.com/demo on your phone. Walk through these 3 beats:

1

Show the merchant dashboard

"This is what you'd see — your customers, check-ins, and rewards all in one place. Real time."

2

Show the customer experience

"This is what your customers see when they scan the QR code. They put in their email or phone number, and that's it. No app download."

3

Close

"The QR code just sits on your counter. Customers check themselves in. You get repeat business automatically."

The close

SAY THIS

"It's $30 a month — or $300 for the year which saves you $60. Flat price, no per-reward fees. Want to set it up now? Takes 5 minutes."

Key framing: Flat pricing means no surprise fees. When a customer hits the reward threshold and comes back 8 times, you pay exactly what you signed up for — nothing more.

Chapter 3
Handling objections

Every "no" is usually just a question in disguise. Here's how to handle the ones you'll hear most.

"We already have a loyalty program."

"That's great — what does it look like right now? Is it paper punch cards?" (Wait for answer.) "Ventzon replaces that automatically. No lost cards, no manual tracking, and you actually get to see who your regulars are by name. Want to see it?"

"We're too busy right now."

"Totally understand — what time is usually slower for you? I can come back then, setup takes 5 minutes. You don't even need to stop what you're doing."

"How much does it cost?"

"$30 a month flat. No per-reward fees, no hidden charges — you know exactly what you pay every month."

"We're not very tech savvy."

"That's exactly who this is built for. You literally just print a QR code and put it on your counter. Customers do everything else — you just watch the dashboard."

"I need to talk to my business partner / spouse."

"Of course. Can I leave you the flyer? And what's the best way for me to follow up — email or phone? I'll send you a link so you can show them the demo."

"Do customers need to download an app?"

"No — that's the best part. They just scan the QR code and enter their phone number or email. That's it. Works on every phone with a camera."

"What if customers don't use it?"

"Most businesses see 20-30% of customers check in within the first week just from curiosity — people love scanning QR codes now. And the reward drives repeat visits automatically."

"Can I cancel anytime?"

"Yes — month to month, no contracts, cancel anytime from your dashboard."

Chapter 4
Onboarding a merchant

Once they say yes, do this with them on the spot. It takes 5 minutes and they'll feel much more confident when you leave.

1

Go to ventzon.com/signup together

Either use their phone or your phone and hand it to them. They create the account with their email and set their shop name.

2

Set up their reward

Ask: "How many visits feels right before they earn something?" (Usually 5–10.) Then: "What's the reward?" Common ones: free item, 10% off, free upgrade. Set this in the onboarding checklist.

3

Set up billing

They enter their card on the Stripe checkout page. Takes 60 seconds. Confirm the subscription is active — their dashboard will show "Pro" status.

4

Download and print the QR code

From their dashboard, download the QR code image. If you have a portable printer — print it on the spot. Otherwise, email it to them and tell them to print it at home or a copy shop and laminate it.

5

Test it with them

Have the owner scan the QR code with their phone, enter their own email, and check in. Show them what the customer sees. Now they can explain it to their own customers.

6

Log them in your rep portal

Go to ventzon.com/rep → Merchants → Log a Merchant. Enter their shop slug (the part of their URL after /merchant/). This links them to your account for commission tracking.

Don't leave without logging them. If you don't log the merchant in your portal before you leave, you won't get credit for the commission.

After you leave

Send them a quick follow-up text within 24 hours. See the templates in Chapter 6. Check in again after week 1 to make sure the QR code is up and they're getting check-ins.
