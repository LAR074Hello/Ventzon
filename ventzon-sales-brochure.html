OVERVIEW

What is Ventzon?

Ventzon is a digital loyalty rewards platform built for local businesses. You replace the paper punch card with a QR code — customers scan it on their phone, earn stamps, and get rewarded automatically. No hardware. No app download needed to check in.

THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

01

Paper cards don't work

Customers lose them, forget them, or switch shops. There's no way to know who your regulars are or how often they come back.

02

Apps have too much friction

Most loyalty apps require a download, account creation, and setup time. 80% of people won't bother. Ventzon works with just an email address.

03

You have no customer data

Without a system, you don't know your top customers, how often they visit, or how to reach them. Ventzon changes that from day one.

04

Competitors are gaining ground

Chains and franchises run loyalty programs at scale. Ventzon gives local businesses the same tools — at a fraction of the cost.
